FAQ

Good to know before you order.

How do I order?

All orders, messages and payments go through Fiverr. Pick a package on the gig, or send me a message on Fiverr if you’d like a custom offer first.

Third-party costs, which you pay directly: your Shopify plan, theme licence, paid apps, domain, hosting and stock images.

Yes. Redesigns, custom sections, speed and SEO work, migrations and ongoing store care are all available as custom offers on Fiverr.

Do you build WordPress sites and web apps too?

Yes: WordPress and WooCommerce, business websites, and custom web and hybrid mobile apps built with React or Angular and Python or PHP.

What do I need to get started?

Your logo, brand colours (if you have them), product details and photos, and a few stores you like. If something’s missing, I’ll help you fill the gaps.
