Questions

Before you check out.

Do I pay anything when I check out?

No. Checkout sends me a quote request. Once we’ve agreed the scope, payment is 50% to start and 50% before launch.

What isn’t included in the price?

Third-party costs, which you pay directly: your Shopify plan, theme licence, paid apps, domain, hosting, stock images, and VAT or GST where it applies.

Why are prices different in pounds and Australian dollars?

Each market (US, UK and Australia) has its own fixed price list. Prices are not live exchange-rate conversions, so the price you see is the price you’re quoted.

Can you work on my existing Shopify store?

Yes. Custom features, redesigns, speed and SEO work, migrations and care plans all start from your current store.

How many revisions do I get?

Unlimited revisions within the agreed scope.
