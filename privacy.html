Privacy

This site collects nothing about you.

There are no accounts, tracking or advertising cookies here. It’s a portfolio. All orders and messages happen on Fiverr.

Ordering

The order buttons take you to Fiverr. Anything you share when ordering is handled under Fiverr’s privacy policy.

The quote estimator

If you enter a reference website in the estimator, that address is sent to Microlink, which reads the public page and returns a screenshot and basic details. Nothing you enter is stored by this site. The brief is only copied to your clipboard for you to paste on Fiverr.

Third-party services

Fonts load from Google Fonts, some portfolio images from Cloudinary, and animation libraries from cdnjs and jsDelivr. These services see standard request data such as your IP address.
