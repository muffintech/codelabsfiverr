Privacy

What happens to your information.

Short version: only what you type into the quote form is sent, it goes to Codelabs by email, and it is used to reply to you.

Quote requests

The checkout form sends your name, email, optional website and country, your message and the items in your cart to Codelabs through FormSubmit, which forwards it by email. You receive an automatic copy. No payment details are collected and nothing is charged.

Currency detection

To show prices in the right currency, the site asks ipapi.co which country your IP address is in. If that fails, it guesses from your device’s time zone. The result is not stored by Codelabs.

Stored on your device

  • Your cart, so it is still there if you come back.
  • Your chosen currency, if you change it.

Both live in your browser’s local storage and can be cleared at any time. There are no tracking or advertising cookies on this site.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts, portfolio images from Cloudinary, and animation libraries from cdnjs and jsDelivr. These services see standard request data such as your IP address.

Contact

To ask about or delete anything you’ve sent, email info@codelabsstorefront.com.
